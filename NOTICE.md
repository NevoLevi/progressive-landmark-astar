# Third-party data and materials

## Moving AI MAPF Benchmark

This artifact redistributes a checksum-pinned snapshot of public Moving AI
benchmark maps and scenarios. The source snapshot, upstream URLs, checksums,
attribution, and license URI are preserved in
[`data/source/movingai_mapf_2021-06-17/PROVENANCE.md`](data/source/movingai_mapf_2021-06-17/PROVENANCE.md).

> Contains information from the Moving AI MAPF Benchmark, which is made
> available under the ODC Attribution License.

License: [Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).
The benchmark is described by Stern et al., *Multi-Agent Pathfinding:
Definitions, Variants, and Benchmarks*, SoCS 2019,
[doi:10.1609/socs.v10i1.18510](https://doi.org/10.1609/socs.v10i1.18510).

## AAAI 2027 author-kit files

The working archive used the unmodified `aaai2027.sty` and `aaai2027.bst` from
the official AAAI 2027 author kit. Their download URL and exact archive/file
hashes are recorded in
[`report/AAAI27_AUTHOR_KIT_PROVENANCE.md`](report/AAAI27_AUTHOR_KIT_PROVENANCE.md).
The public release does **not** redistribute `aaai2027.sty`; retrieve the
checksum-pinned official kit from AAAI before rebuilding the report. This
boundary follows AAAI's
[website copyright notice](https://aaai.org/about-aaai/copyright-notice/).
`aaai2027.bst` remains included under the LaTeX Project Public License stated
in its header.
